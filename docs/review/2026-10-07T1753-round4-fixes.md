These are the round-4 review's "after v0.1" items. They are unchanged and still listed there:

Residuals introduced by these fixes, all documented:
- **8-bit namespace tag collisions:** a collision (1/255) between two distinct namespaces falls back to the old pid-only judgment.
- **Foreign crashed claims stay stuck:** a crashed in-flight claim in another namespace leaves that one slot unusable until `reap`, under the same never-steal rule as identities.
- **One reservation per producer instance:** callers sharing a producer across async tasks must serialize their reserve→commit sections. The losing `reserveAsync` gets `E_RING_STATE` instead of silently corrupting the ring.
